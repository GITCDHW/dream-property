About This House in Kalipur, Dankuni

Situated just a 2-minute walk from the bustling Kalipur 4-Matha More in Dankuni, this expansive 5 Katha independent property is a turnkey rental investment. Built on clear Bastu land (fully eligible for bank loans), the compound features 4 entirely separate housing structures. The front-facing room is specifically designed to function as a commercial shop or office space. Inside, multiple pre-leased and vacant units offer immediate monthly income generation. The rear of the property opens up to a tranquil private pond view and ample open land planted with mango, papaya, and coconut trees—perfect for nature lovers or cultivating a large kitchen garden.
